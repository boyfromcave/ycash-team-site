YecWallet

The Ycash wallet for your computer. This version adds a Yellowback tab for YED, a dollar on Ycash.

YecWallet 6.22.0-beta3 is a pre-release for testing. It brings its own node, ycashd, so there is nothing else to install. Apple silicon Macs get macos-arm64-yecwallet-v6.22.0-beta3.dmg; other systems are built when possible and may be missing.

Download YecWallet

Check the download

The app is not signed or notarized by Apple. Our own signature is what shows the file is genuine. Download SHA256SUMS and SHA256SUMS.minisig from the same release, then in Terminal, in that folder:

shasum -a 256 -c --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS
brew install minisign
minisign -Vm SHA256SUMS -P RWQYr5PFpiPJAigk86vf89bPYrJCXj5tskgRAPRYFsEXyciZo8dveOGQ

Expect OK next to your file, then Signature and comment signature verified. This is the Ycash release key (id 02C923A6C593AF18) that also signs ycashd. If a check fails, delete the file and do not open it.

Open it the first time on a Mac

  1. Open the .dmg and drag yecwallet onto Applications.
  2. Open yecwallet. macOS says Apple could not verify it. Click Done, not Move to Trash.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security and click Open Anyway. Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click Open Anyway once more.

Right-click → Open no longer skips this. The button shows for about an hour after the blocked attempt. A Mac managed by an organization may not offer it at all. The full guide is in the YecWallet repository.
